# Smart Insole

## Register

product

## Users

The researcher operating two capacitive smart insoles from a Windows laptop in a lit lab.
They watch the participant and must recognize connection, recording and calibration state at a glance.

## Product Purpose

Local-only acquisition over UDP, unloaded pressure baseline, stationary IMU calibration,
reliable raw CSV recording and CNN analysis after stopping. The existing native app remains available.

## Brand Personality

Clear, bright, precise. Indonesian interface copy with short action labels.

## References and Anti-references

The user explicitly requests a bright blue interface, the mobile app's heatmaps and trends,
MBUGM's Plus Jakarta Sans / Fraunces typography, and visual references from Awwwards UI Design.
Avoid the mobile app's excess explanatory text, decorative KPI tiles, placeholder metrics,
repeated cards, noisy gradients and marketing hero sections in an acquisition tool.

## Design Principles

- The bilateral footprint and sensor timeline are the subject, not decoration.
- Keep receive, calibration, recording and post-record analysis state independent and visible.
- Show missing/invalid measurements honestly; never synthesize a missing foot.
- Preserve original measurements and version derived calibration separately.
- Details belong in disclosures; show guidance when an action needs it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, text states alongside colors, numeric alternatives
to heatmaps and graphs, reduced-motion support and responsive laptop/tablet layouts.
